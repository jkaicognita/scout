    04 — How a pilot runs

    Six weeks from brief to decision.

    1. Week 0

      Brief

      90-minute problem framing with your stakeholders. We agree on constraints and what "success" means.

    2. Weeks 1–2

      Scout

      Landscape, scoring and vendor calls. You get a shortlist with a clear rationale for each pick.

    3. Weeks 2–5

      Pilot

      Hands-on proof-of-concept with real data, measured against the baseline set on day one.

    4. Week 6

      Decide

      Decision memo, business case and rollout or exit plan. No sunk-cost pilots that drag on.
